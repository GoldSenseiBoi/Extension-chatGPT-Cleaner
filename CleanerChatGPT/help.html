✅ Steps to Retrieve the Bearer Token in Chrome

  1. Go to https://chatgpt.com
    → Make sure you are logged into your OpenAI account.
  2. Open DevTools (F12 or right-click > Inspect)
  3. Go to the Network tab.
  4. Reload the page (F5)
  5. In the list of requests, look for or filter a line to: 
    https://chatgpt.com/backend-api/conversation
  6. Click on it → Go to the Headers tab.
  7. Scroll down to the Request Headers section.
  8. You will see a line like: 
    Authorization: Bearer eybiOi...
  9. ✅ Copy the part after "Bearer" (that’s the token).
